Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026

This website, casanovanjpa.com, is operated by Nicholas Rodriguez Jr., a licensed real estate salesperson (NJ #1969622, PA #RS369752) with Keller Williams Realty in Moorestown, New Jersey. This policy explains what personal information is collected through this website, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

What is collected

Information you give directly. When you use the contact form, book a call, email or telephone, we collect your name, email address, telephone number, and whatever you choose to tell us about the property you are buying or selling.

Scheduling. Appointments are booked through Cal.com, which collects your name, email address and selected time and passes them to us. Cal.com's own privacy policy governs their handling of that data.

Analytics. If you accept cookies, this site uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies and records your IP address, device and browser type, the pages you view and how you arrived. This measures traffic in aggregate; it is not used to identify you personally. If you reject cookies, Google Analytics does not load.

Server logs. Our website host records standard log information, including IP address and time of request.

How it is used

To answer your enquiry and provide real estate brokerage services; to schedule and confirm appointments; to send property information you have asked for; to measure and improve the website; and to meet the record-keeping obligations that apply to licensed real estate professionals in New Jersey and Pennsylvania.

We do not sell your personal information

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Who it is shared with

Keller Williams Realty, the brokerage under which all transactions are conducted. Service providers who run parts of this site for us: our website host (GitHub Pages), our form delivery service, Cal.com for scheduling, YouTube when you choose to play a video, and Google Analytics. Mortgage or title companies, only when you ask to be referred to one. Anyone else only at your direction, or where the law requires it.

Cookies

This site uses cookies only for analytics, and only after you accept them. You can block or delete them in your browser settings and the site will still work. Google also offers a browser add-on to opt out of Google Analytics entirely. Videos load from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain and only when you press play.

How long it is kept

Enquiry and transaction records are kept for as long as needed to provide services and to satisfy the record-retention rules that apply to licensed real estate professionals, and are then deleted.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information held about you, or ask us to stop contacting you. Email casanovaforsale@gmail.com or call 856-888-4334 and we will respond promptly. Depending on where you live you may have further rights under state privacy law.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Fair housing

All real estate services are offered without regard to race, color, religion, sex, handicap, familial status, or national origin.

Changes

This policy is reviewed and updated when our practices change, and the effective date above is revised.
