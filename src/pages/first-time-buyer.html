Do I have to be a first-time buyer to use New Jersey's programs?

For NJHMFA's programs, a first-time buyer is someone who hasn't owned their main home in the past three years. In Urban Target Areas, which in Camden County include Camden, Cherry Hill, Pennsauken, Gloucester City, Berlin, Pine Hill and Woodlynne, buyers don't have to be first-time buyers as long as they won't own another main home at closing. Qualified veterans don't need to be first-time buyers either.

How much is the down payment help in Camden County?

Up to $10,000 toward the down payment and closing costs, as a loan with no interest and no monthly payment that is forgiven after five years if you keep living in the home and don't refinance or sell. Income and price limits apply, and you apply through a participating NJHMFA lender. Source: NJHMFA fact sheet updated June 17, 2026.
