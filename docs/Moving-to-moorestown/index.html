Before you go

Run the monthly payment with Burlington County taxes.

Moorestown is in Burlington County, which averages about 2.65% a year in property tax. On a $500,000 home that is roughly $13,250 a year, or about $1,100 a month on top of the mortgage. Your actual bill depends on the assessment.
